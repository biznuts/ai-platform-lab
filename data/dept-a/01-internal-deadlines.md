# Internal Grant Deadlines

> Fictional sample document for a lab demo. Not a real policy of any institution.

Applications to external funders must reach the Research Grants Office (RGO) at least **10 business days** before the funder's deadline. This gives RGO time to check the budget, eligibility and signatures.

Late submissions are reviewed only if capacity allows, and the applicant must accept the risk that errors will not be caught.
