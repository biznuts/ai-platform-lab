# Expense Claim Deadlines

> Fictional sample document for a lab demo. Not a real policy of any institution.

Expense claims must reach the Finance Office within **30 calendar days** of the expense, or of the last day of the trip. Each claim needs itemized receipts and the approval of the claimant's manager.

Claims older than 90 days are paid only with the department head's written approval. Finance does not reimburse claims older than one year.
